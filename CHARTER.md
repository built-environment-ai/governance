## 1. Mandate & Scope

Built Environment AI Initiative is an independent open-science research initiative dedicated to advancing computational architecture, Building Information Modelling (BIM) interoperability, and machine learning applications for building energy and environmental performance.

The initiative produces peer-reviewed research, reproducible simulation pipelines, open datasets, and transparent evidence syntheses that bridge early-stage architectural design with physics-based building performance simulation.

## 2. Core Methodological Principles

All projects conducted under `built-environment-ai` adhere to four foundational principles:

1. **Interpretable & Physics-Informed Modeling:** Surrogate models and predictive workflows must prioritize physical consistency and explainability (e.g., SHAP feature attribution, sensitivity analysis, uncertainty bounds) over opaque black-box accuracy.
2. **Resource-Frugal Computational Design:** Methodologies should remain accessible to practitioners and researchers operating without hyperscale computing infrastructure, favoring lean tabular/geometric learning, structured BIM schemas, and reproducible open-source toolchains.
3. **Human-Centered Engineering Agency:** Computational tools are designed to eliminate repetitive technical friction while preserving architectural authorship, engineering judgment, and manual override capability.
4. **Radical Reproducibility:** Every empirical study links openly versioned protocols, screening logs, data schemas, and execution scripts prior to or alongside manuscript dissemination.

## 3. Project Registry & Lifecycle Standards

Every research workstream is assigned a sequential project identifier (`P001`, `P002`, `P003`, ...) and hosted in a dedicated public repository following the naming convention `p<NNN>-<short-topic-slug>`.

Each project progresses through five documented lifecycle states:
- **`Protocol`**: Research questions, search strategy, or experimental design registered prior to data extraction or model training.
- **`Active`**: Data collection, simulation runs, or synthesis actively underway.
- **`Preprint`**: Complete manuscript and reproducible artifacts deposited on an open preprint server.
- **`Published`**: Peer-reviewed journal or conference publication linked with archival DOI.
- **`Archived`**: Completed project maintained in read-only reference state.

### Active Registry
- **`P001`**: `p001-ai-bim-energy-scoping-review` — Scoping review protocol and open synthesis dataset on AI-BIM integration for early-stage building energy performance prediction (`Protocol`).

## 4. Authorship & Collaboration

Contributors to any initiative repository are credited transparently using the **CRediT (Contributor Roles Taxonomy)** standard in the project `README.md` and resulting manuscripts. External academic and industry collaborations are welcomed via `contact@built-environment-ai.org`.
